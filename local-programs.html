Grosse Pointe · Harper Woods

Local Youth Sports Programs

Community organizations that took part in the Raising Athletes, Strengthening Communities effort.

The organizations below are local programs our planning team knows as supportive community resources. Most also responded directly to our outreach and contributed information to this guide. Together, they represent a range of sports, ages, and levels of participation, with opportunities for children to learn, compete, develop, and stay connected to sport close to home.

Softball

Grosse Pointe Little League Softball

Community-based softball for girls across the Grosse Pointe area, with spring and fall programming across multiple age divisions. GPLL Softball also provides coaching tools and resources to support volunteer coaches and player development.

grossepointesoftball.com · Website & registration
Baseball & softball

Grosse Pointe Woods-Shores Little League

Serves Grosse Pointe Woods, Grosse Pointe Shores, and Harper Woods with youth baseball and softball opportunities. The league emphasizes player development, supports multisport athletes, welcomes both experienced players and children new to the game, and provides instruction and training resources for new and veteran coaches.

grossepointelittleleague.com · Website & registration
Swimming

Grosse Pointe Gators

A year-round competitive swim program offering skill-appropriate training groups for swimmers at different stages of development. The program provides structured instruction and competitive opportunities while supporting athletes as they progress through the sport.

gpgatorsswim.org · Website
Swimming · Summer

Grosse Pointe Woods Warriors Swim Team

A summer recreational swim team through the City of Grosse Pointe Woods and the Lakefront Swimming Association. The program emphasizes proper stroke technique and an appreciation for swimming, with age-group practices, weekly dual meets, and developmental mini-meets for swimmers 12 and under.

gpwmi.us · Program information
Field hockey

Great Lakes Regional Field Hockey

A nonprofit organization serving and supporting the growth of field hockey at the local level. Programs welcome athletes of varying ages and abilities through high-quality coaching, skill development, fitness training, and play, while also supporting coach education and broader access to the sport.

glrfieldhockey.org · Website
Tennis & pickleball

Court4 Tennis & Pickleball

Offers year-round tennis and pickleball opportunities, including group classes, private instruction, and court rentals. Junior programming gives young athletes opportunities to learn, practice, and develop their skills in a year-round environment.

court4.club · Junior programs
Soccer

EastSide FC

A community-based soccer organization serving the Grosse Pointes and surrounding area through recreational and select programming. EastSide provides opportunities for beginners, multisport athletes, and players seeking a higher competitive level, with an emphasis on skill development, teamwork, and sportsmanship.

eastfc.org · Website
Multi-sport & recreation

Neighborhood Club

A Grosse Pointe community nonprofit offering recreation, social, and wellness programming. Youth opportunities span multiple sports and activities, creating local entry points for children of different ages, interests, and experience levels.

neighborhoodclub.org · Youth programs & registration

How endorsement works

Local organizations included in this edition engaged with or supported the Raising Athletes, Strengthening Communities effort. Their inclusion is not intended as a ranking, certification, or endorsement of one program over another, nor does the absence of an organization imply a negative assessment.
